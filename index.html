Questions

Frequently asked

How long does container shipping to Nigeria take?

It depends on where your cargo is coming from and the sailing schedule. We give you an estimated arrival date with your quote, and you can follow every stage with your tracking number.

Can I ship a car and other goods in the same container?

Yes. Many customers ship a vehicle together with auto parts or personal goods. Tell us everything you want to ship and we'll plan the load.

How do I track my shipment?

When your booking is confirmed you get a DMP tracking number, like DMP-123456. Enter it in the tracking box on this page to see where your container is.

Which container size should I choose?

Use the container finder above for a quick guide. As a rule, a 20ft suits 1–2 cars, a 40ft suits 3–4, and a 40ft High Cube is best for tall vehicles and bulky items. We'll confirm the right size with your quote.

Can you buy a vehicle for me?

Yes. Tell us the make, model, year and budget. We find the vehicle, share the details with you, buy it and ship it.

What documents do I need?

It depends on what you're shipping and where. When you request a quote, we'll tell you exactly which documents your shipment needs.
